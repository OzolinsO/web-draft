02 / 14 · How it worksTelemetry up. Plan down. Every 15 minutes.

A HeloGrid controller is installed at each site. It collects telemetry such as power, voltage, temperature and charge level directly from the equipment over the building's local network, without going through any manufacturer's cloud.

The telemetry is sent securely to the HeloGrid cloud. There it is combined with electricity prices, weather forecasts and the site's tariff. The cloud calculates the best operating plan for the day and sends it back to the controller, which carries it out.

All telemetry is stored, so it can feed alerts, maintenance planning, performance analysis and client reports.

01

Collect

The controller reads telemetry from inverters, batteries, heat pumps, EV chargers, meters and controllable loads, using the communication standard each device supports.

02

Transfer

Telemetry is sent to the cloud over an encrypted connection. If the internet drops, it is kept locally and sent once the connection is back.

03

Enrich

The cloud adds day-ahead electricity prices loaded from many power exchanges and marketplaces, weather and solar forecasts, and the site's own tariff.

04

Plan

It calculates a schedule for the battery, heat pumps and chargers, always within the site's grid limits.

05

Execute

The controller carries out the plan locally and keeps following it even if the connection is lost.

06

Store and report

Stored telemetry history drives fault detection, maintenance tracking and monthly reports.

Lower energy costs through coordinated control.

05 / 14 · Energy costsEvery device on one plan. Savings measured monthly.

HeloGrid does not only monitor equipment. It is a full energy management system that controls the equipment so that consumption follows electricity prices and the site's tariff. The savings are measured and reported every month, so the client can weigh them against the cost of the service.

  • Price-aware battery useThe battery charges in cheap hours and covers consumption in expensive ones, based on day-ahead prices for each 15 minutes.
  • More value from solarSurplus solar is stored instead of sold cheaply. Export can stop automatically when market prices turn negative.
  • Lower peak chargesGrid import is kept under a peak target, which reduces capacity fees on commercial sites.
  • Flexible loadsEV charging and heat pumps shift to cheaper or sunnier hours. Comfort ranges and "ready by" times are always respected.
  • A goal per siteChoose lowest total cost, maximum own solar use, or backup first.
  • Transparent savingsSavings are calculated against buying the same electricity from the grid at the same prices. Figures are marked as estimates where the tariff is incomplete.

Years of operating data, ready to work with.

08 / 14 · Data platformEvery value kept. Ready for a digital twin.

Every site sends a continuous stream of telemetry from every device. HeloGrid stores it in a purpose-built, layered database architecture: time-series storage for measurements, a structured model of every device and how the site is wired, and a complete log of events and commands, all linked together. Every device is described the same way, whatever the brand. This structure drives the reports, alerts and plan today, and is designed from the start as the foundation of a digital twin: a live virtual model of each site.

  • One data model for every brandEquipment from different makers is stored in the same structure, so sites compare like for like.
  • Full history at 15-minute resolutionEvery measurement is kept, not summarised away after a few weeks.
  • Built for a digital twinDevice data, wiring and operating history are stored together, following industrial digital twin standards, so each site can be modelled virtually to test changes and size upgrades.
  • Compare across the portfolioFind the weakest inverters, the fastest-ageing batteries or the sites closest to their grid limits.
  • Proof when it mattersA time-stamped record of measurements, commands and settings supports warranty claims, disputes and audits.
  • Your data, exportableAny period can be exported for consultants, auditors or your own tools.

Grid limits and operator requirements, enforced on site.

09 / 14 · Grid safetyLimits hold, even offline.

Every site must respect the limits of its grid connection and, increasingly, follow requests from the grid operator. HeloGrid applies these on the controller itself, so they hold even when the site is offline. Every plan and every command works inside them.

  • Connection limitsImport limit, export limit and main fuse size are taken from the connection permit. Neither the installer nor the site owner can override them.
  • Grid operator commandsCommands from the distribution system operator are followed automatically within the required response time.
  • Clear prioritiesCommands from the grid operator or a virtual power plant operator take priority over the HeloGrid plan, but never over safety limits.
  • Grid reportPeak demand, limit breaches, operator requests, outages and power quality are reported against the European voltage quality standard EN 50160.
  • Backup during power cutsSites with a battery and the right switchgear can run on their own battery when the grid is down.

Secure by design. Independent of manufacturer clouds.

10 / 14 · Security and reliabilityLocal control. EU hosted. Verified connections.

Control of energy equipment must not depend on a third party's cloud or on an internet connection. HeloGrid controls devices locally, keeps working offline, and protects every connection between site and server.

  • Hosted in the EUThe platform runs on servers located in the European Union.
  • Local controlThe controller talks to each device directly over the local network. No manufacturer cloud is involved.
  • Keeps running offlineThe site follows its stored plan and limits. Telemetry is buffered and sent in order once the connection returns.
  • Industrial messaging standardTelemetry and commands follow Eclipse Sparkplug, the open industrial standard for MQTT. Every value arrives in a defined structure, and the platform knows immediately when a controller goes offline or comes back.
  • Mutually verified connectionsWith mTLS, the controller and the server each check the other's certificate before exchanging any data.
  • Separate credentials per controllerEach controller has its own MQTT login. If one is compromised, no other site is affected.
  • Full change logEvery settings change is recorded with who made it and when.
